My purchase is gone after changing phones

Open Menu → Settings → "Trial & Purchase" → "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Game records (your kokedama, Collection, photos) are stored only on your old device and cannot be moved.

Is there a subscription?

No. There is a free 14-day trial and a single one-time purchase. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

Why does it rain on my porch when it's sunny here?

Outside Japan, "Surprise Weather" follows the skies of Nagano, Japan, so rain or snow arrives by surprise. You can turn it off in Settings.

How do I change the language?

Menu → Settings → Language: Auto, 日本語, or English. The change applies the next time you open the app.
