FAQ

Do my photos go into the Photos app?

No. Photos you take are saved only inside JobShot Box. To keep some in the Photos app, select them in a box and tap “Save.”

How are photos sorted into boxes?

Photos taken in a row go into the same box. After a break, the next photo starts a new box. You can change the length of the break in Settings, name boxes later, and move photos between boxes.

I deleted a box by mistake. Can I get it back?

Boxes marked as done go to Trash and can be restored for 7 days. After that they are deleted automatically. Photos deleted one by one, and boxes deleted permanently from Trash, can’t be restored.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos and box names stay on your device. There is no account and no ads. Deleting the app also deletes its photos, so save or share anything you want to keep first.

What does Premium do?

Without Premium you can sort into up to 3 boxes; photos beyond that go into one “Unsorted” box. Premium is a one-time purchase that lets you sort into as many boxes as you like. There is never a limit on the number of photos, and shooting never stops.

I changed phones. How do I get Premium back?

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore Purchase,” signed in with the same App Store account you used to buy it.

Can I open the app from the Camera Control button or the Lock Screen?

Yes, on iOS 18 or later. Go to Settings → Camera → Camera Control and choose JobShot Box, or add the JobShot Box button in Control Center or on the Lock Screen. You can switch back anytime. The built-in Camera app keeps working as usual.

The app shows Japanese. How do I switch to English?

The app follows your iPhone’s language: Japanese if it is set to Japanese, English otherwise. To choose the language yourself, open Settings in the app and pick “English” under “言語 / Language.” The app restarts to switch; your photos and boxes stay as they are.
