Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

JobShot Box does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The photos you take, the names of your boxes (jobs), and the App's settings are all stored only on your device.

2. How Your Data Is Handled

Your photos and box names are stored only on your device and are never sent to any third party, including the developer. However, when it starts, the App contacts the servers of Expo (an app delivery service run by Expo, a company in the United States) to check for updates. Only the information needed for this check (such as the App version and device type) is sent; your photos and box names are never sent. The only other communication is with the app store when you purchase or restore Premium (see "7. Payment Information"). In airplane mode, every feature works except checking for updates and purchasing or restoring. When you delete the App, the photos stored in it are also deleted from your device. If you back up your iPhone or Android device, this data is included in that backup.

3. Use of the Camera

The App uses the camera to take photos for your work. Photos you take are stored only inside the App and are not automatically saved to your device's Photos app.

4. Your Device's Photos App

The App never reads the contents of your device’s Photos app. Only when you choose photos yourself and tap "Save to Photos" does the App write those photos to the Photos app. This is the only time you will be asked for permission to save.

5. Sharing Photos With Other Apps

Only when you choose to share do the selected photos go to the app you pick (email, messages, cloud storage, and so on). After that, they are handled under that service's terms. The developer never sees them. Shared photos include shooting information (Exif) such as the date, time, and device model. Because the App does not access your location, the place where a photo was taken is not included.

6. Third Parties and Advertising

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics tools. We do not provide any information to third parties.

7. Payment Information

When you purchase or restore Premium, the App communicates with your device's app store (Apple or Google), and the store processes the payment. The App never receives or stores payment details such as credit card numbers. Only whether you have purchased Premium is stored on your device.

8. Information Received When You Contact Us

If you contact us, the developer receives your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply to you and do not provide them to third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy in response to changes in laws or in the App's features. When we do, we will post the revised Policy and its effective date in the App and on our public page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Policy, please contact arima.app.support@gmail.com.
