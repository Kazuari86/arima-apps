Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kokedama Days (the "App") does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your kokedama, points, album photos, Home View photos, and settings are all stored only on your device.

The birthday (month and day) you may enter for "Today's Fortune" is also stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. Entering it is optional; everything else in the App works without it.

The developer only receives personal information when you contact us by email yourself (see section 8).

2. How Your Data Is Handled

Your records and photos are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or any third party. The App has no developer-operated server. If you delete the App, its records and in-app photos are deleted from your device and cannot be restored by the developer (images you saved to your Photos remain).

3. Device Features

The App asks for permission only when a feature is first needed, never all at once at launch.

  • Notifications: to schedule local notifications on your device about your kokedama and your morning fortune (asked when you turn notifications on).
  • Steps: to give in-game points for the steps you walk, the App reads your daily step count (Motion & Fitness on iPhone). Only daily step totals are read; no location or route is used, and no Health data is read.
  • Camera: to take a photo for the Home View background.
  • Photos: to save kokedama photos to your Photos and to pick a photo for the background. Picked photos are processed and kept inside the App, and never sent anywhere.
  • Weather: outside Japan, "Surprise Weather" is on by default. To make rain and snow appear now and then, the App gets the forecast for Nagano, Japan from the Japan Meteorological Agency. Only a number identifying Nagano is sent; no location is used. If you turn Surprise Weather off in Settings, this connection is never made. (In Japan, the App instead uses the prefecture you choose, and connects only if you choose one.)

When you share a photo, the App simply hands the image to your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The App does not use location, the microphone, or contacts.

4. Third Parties, Ads, and Analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. We do not provide any information to third parties.

5. In-App Purchases

Starting the free trial and making a purchase are handled through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is never received by the developer. Purchase status is checked between your device and the store.

6. Children's Privacy

The App can be used by people of any age, and it does not collect personal information from users of any age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features are added or laws change. If we do, we will announce the new content and its effective date on this page before it takes effect.
